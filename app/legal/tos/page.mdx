## 5. Data Retention and Credential Handling

**TuringTap retains no traffic data.** Decrypted request and response content
exists only in volatile per-session memory on the proxy and is purged when
your Session ends. We do not persist request bodies, headers, credentials, or
structural metadata about your traffic — except content you or your AI agent
explicitly author into a handoff request (the prompt text shown to you),
which we store until the handoff expires. We retain only your account record
(email, subscription tier, hashed API keys, Stripe customer reference) and
per-session byte counters used for quota enforcement, which are deleted with
the Session.

During a live Session, in-memory traffic is made available to **your** AI
Client via the `get_traffic()` MCP tool, which transmits it — including any
credentials it contains, in raw form — to **your** AI Provider over **their**
API. **We do not filter or redact it:** partial redaction invites false
confidence, so we do not promise it. Your AI Provider's data-retention and
training policies — not ours — govern what happens to it there. TuringTap has
no visibility into or control over your AI Provider's logs.

**If credentials you consider sensitive transited a Session, rotate them when
you are done.** Rotating them is your responsibility.
